About System

Advanced Crop Protection
Through Deep Learning

Plant diseases cause significant economic losses in agriculture worldwide. Early detection and accurate diagnosis are crucial for effective pest management and crop protection. Our system leverages Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) trained on the vast PlantVillage dataset.

The model processes images of plant leaves, extracts intricate visual features like spots, discoloration, and mold, and compares them against known disease signatures. This allows for millisecond-level predictions that can help farmers take immediate corrective actions, reducing crop yield loss and minimizing the unnecessary use of pesticides.
